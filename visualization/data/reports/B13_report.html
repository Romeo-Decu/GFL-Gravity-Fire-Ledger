What this report does not establish

  • The position of the burning (pyrolysis) front: only the luminous edge is measured, and their offset may change during a run.
  • The flow speed at the sample surface: the calibration is on the duct centreline.
  • Agreement with NASA's own analysis: results stay open until compared with the official challenge data.
  • Review by an external human expert in combustion.
