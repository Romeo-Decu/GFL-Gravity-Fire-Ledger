2. Result

2.01 mm/s

Reported interval 1.82–2.12 mm/s, at air display 2 (2.01 cm/s on the duct centreline by NASA's fan calibration). This is a luminous-edge spread rate (DEC-056): the speed of the flame's luminous edge, taken at 0.5 of each frame's peak brightness, under the frozen window rule R-W4.

What this report does not establish

  • The position of the burning (pyrolysis) front: only the luminous edge is measured, and their offset may change during a run.
  • The flow speed at the sample surface: the calibration is on the duct centreline.
  • Run-to-run scatter: this condition was flown once.

These are limits of the public archive, not of the method: the data that would settle them are not in it.
