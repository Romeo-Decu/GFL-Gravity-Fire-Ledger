# Earth applications

**Status:** specified, not built (DEC-071).

## Problem
Microgravity removes buoyancy, which exposes the slow, low-flow burning that on Earth hides inside larger fires: smouldering and near-limit flames in confined, poorly ventilated spaces (cable trays, battery enclosures, aircraft holds, submarines). The question: which microgravity findings may inform these Earth cases, and under what stated limits.

## Design
- **The same three questions** (relevance, comparability, transferability), with the Earth case described in the comparability vocabulary: material, thickness, oxygen, effective flow, confinement.
- **Direction of transfer reversed:** from flight evidence to a ground scenario, with gravity handled as in the partial-gravity specification.
- **A second use that needs no physics:** the ledger, the frozen rules and the quote-checked extraction work on any experimental archive; the evidence map can be rebuilt over another repository by changing the catalogue.

## Required data
Ground tests matched to a flight run (same material, thickness, oxygen); these are the "matched ground test" of GFL's next research question.

## Validation
One matched pair, predicted before the ground value is read.

## Blocking conditions
No matched ground data in the public archive; GFL makes no safety claim for any Earth system.
